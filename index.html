What it takes to make the quarterfinals

Win both remaining games in regulation, and win them by a margin. The 18U division is one 18-team table. Everyone plays three games, then the top 8 seeds go to the quarterfinals Saturday night. Roughly 8 to 10 teams usually finish with 6 or more points, so a 6-point team sits right on the seed 7 to 9 bubble and the tiebreaker decides it.

  • Two regulation wins gets us to 6 points. A shootout win is only worth 2, which drops us to 5 and almost certainly a consolation seed.
  • Goal quotient is the tiebreaker among tied teams: goals for ÷ (goals for + goals against). This morning's 0–5 means we start deep in the hole. We need to outscore our next two opponents by a combined 6 or more, or post shutouts, to get back above .500.
  • Shutouts count double. Goals against is in the quotient and is also the next tiebreaker after it.
  • Run it up, to a point. A 7-goal cap applies to prelim differentials, so anything past 7–0 doesn't help the seed.
  • Head to head helps. If we finish tied on points with West Michigan White or Michigan Steel Grey, beating them tonight and tomorrow puts us ahead of them.

The rules that decide seeding

Points. Regulation win 3 · Shootout win 2 · Shootout loss 1 · Regulation loss 0. No ties.

Two teams tied

  1. Head to head
  2. Goal quotient: goals for ÷ (goals for + goals against)
  3. Fewest goals against
  4. Fastest goal
  5. Coin flip

Three or more teams tied

  1. Goal quotient (head to head only breaks equal quotients)
  2. Fewest goals against
  3. Fastest goal
  4. Coin flip

If one tied team beat every other tied team it is seeded first among them, and a team that lost to every other tied team is seeded last. A maximum 7-goal differential applies to all preliminary games. Forfeits are recorded 1–0.
