The rules that decide seeding

Points. Regulation win 3 · Shootout win 2 · Shootout loss 1 · Regulation loss 0. No ties.

Two teams tied

  1. Head to head
  2. Goal quotient: goals for ÷ (goals for + goals against)
  3. Fewest goals against
  4. Fastest goal
  5. Coin flip

Three or more teams tied

  1. Goal quotient (head to head only breaks equal quotients)
  2. Fewest goals against
  3. Fastest goal
  4. Coin flip

If one tied team beat every other tied team it is seeded first among them, and a team that lost to every other tied team is seeded last. A maximum 7-goal differential applies to all preliminary games. Forfeits are recorded 1–0.
